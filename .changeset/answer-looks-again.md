`ask symbol`, on `variance ask`, `variance serve`, `variance-authority-help` and its server, and `docs_search` over either server, look at the files their answer names: when one changed or went away after the generation, the question is read again before it is answered, so a renamed export is no longer given under its old name and a declaration and its members are given at the lines the file holds today. An answer whose named files are unchanged keeps its `Snapshot <T>.`. When the checkout cannot be read again, the answer stands and says it was not compared, and why. `serve` from `@variance-authority/mcp` takes `outlived`, which sends an answered call to `again`, and `@variance-authority/help` exports `outlived`.
