`ask search` weighs a word a doc holds at most two thirds as much as the same word in a name, however short the doc. A row's weight is multiplied by how much of the question it holds, each word by its rarity, so a long doc holding most of the question sorts among the first rows, above short names holding two of its words. A name with more than one row — published by more than one package, or published and also exported — is weighed through one of them, by the words its name and that row's doc hold and by that doc's length, and shown with that row: the row whose doc adds the most different words of the question to those its name holds, then, of rows adding as many, the shorter doc, then a published row before an exported one, then the row read first. A name no doc adds a word to is weighed by the doc of the row it is shown with. Asked with `from` or `to`, a name is counted only through a row the section shows there: a published row a file of the area imports, or an exported row of a file in the area.
