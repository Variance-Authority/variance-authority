A journey no longer places a call by its name. A method call on an object the source does not trace, and a `new` the source does not resolve, used to be drawn to the one function the case ran under that name, or to the first constructor it ran of a class so named. Both were namesakes, not targets: they now have no place, and `name-match` and `new` are gone from the ways a call is known. Journeys prepared before this are prepared again.
