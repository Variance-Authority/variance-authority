A story run keeps each change a test line made to the page as one line of text: the node added, removed or changed, with its role, id and name, the component that rendered it and the React commit that wrote it. `variance story --page <text>` answers when something appeared or went with one row per change that holds the text, however large the page, and `--compare` sets apart a change that lands at another commit on the failing runs. What a field holds is never written.
