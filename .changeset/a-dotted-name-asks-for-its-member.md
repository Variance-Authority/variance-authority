`ask symbol` and `ask uses` answer a name written as `Container.member` with the member that container declares, where they refused it as undeclared. A name a file also declares at its top level lists its members beside that declaration, where the declaration hid them.

So does a name imported by the path of its file, and a published one. The first five member declarations are listed and the rest counted. A container that declares the member in no body of its own is said to, with where the member is declared and that which members it inherits or merges is TypeScript's answer.
