`ask search` reads a doc as writing a question of several words when one of its paragraphs holds them in order by stem, each as many words after the last as in the question, so `page sink` finds `eventCollectorSource`, whose doc installs "the page's sink". A word the question is put in, such as `of`, holds its place, so "a page and its sink" no longer writes `page sink`, either as a match or in how near the described names sort. `phraseOfNative` answers the phrase, and `nearOfNative` counts a doc's pairs by the same rule.
