`ask symbol` and `ask uses`, on `variance ask`, `variance serve`, `variance-authority-help` and its server, and `docs_search` over either server, look at the files their answer rests on: when one changed or went away after the generation, the question is read again before it is answered, so a renamed export is no longer given under its old name and a declaration and its members are given at the lines the file holds today. An answer whose files are unchanged keeps its `Snapshot <T>.`. When the checkout cannot be read again, or a file the answer rests on is saved again during that read, the answer stands and says it was not compared, and why, on its one date line. `serve` from `@variance-authority/mcp` takes `outlived`, which sends an answered call to `again`; `again` is given the call's arguments and may return `unseen`, which reaches the tool on `ToolInvocation`. `@variance-authority/help` exports `outlived`. A generation now says when its read began, on `readStartedAt` beside `generatedAt`, and that is the time the files are compared with, so a file saved during the read counts as changed. `updateSourceIndex` answers it, `publishWorkspaceFromIndex` takes it, a refresh of `--changed-file` keeps the time of the read it refreshed, and a value published before it is refused and read again once. A file written after the read gives no text of today's: a declaration and its members are listed by the names recorded, on every path including `--just-answer`.
