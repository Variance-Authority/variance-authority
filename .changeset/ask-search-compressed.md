`variance ask search` answers in fewer lines. When the repository answers, the installed names and the packages that describe your words are each one line naming up to five and the question that lists them in full, which a `--limit` you say does too, and the line saying no installed name matched is left out; a suite whose recorded cases were not read is named on one line, and only when the repository answered with no row; the near-match and loose headings are one short line; and an answer holding a row of this repository ends with `Next:` and the question for its first declaration, or for its first recorded test case when no declaration answered, spelled as you ask: a command in the shell, a tool and its input over MCP. Each list shows at most 20 published rows and 10 of every other kind unless `--limit` says more.
