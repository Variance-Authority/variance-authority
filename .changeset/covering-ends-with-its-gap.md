`variance covering` ends an answer that leaves its question open with a gap: the question, the scope, what is known, the reason (`absent`, `stale` or `coarse`), the exact commands that close it, and how many tests in how many test files they run. A line held at file grain names the story of the three nearest tests (Deep). A record of another text, lines written or ranges edited since the recording, a record older than the file, and no record at all name a recorded run (Record). The gap is printed after the answer; under `--format json` it is the answer's `gap`, and it sits beside `refused` in a refusal. The skill's new `fsd` reference says which reading answers a question and what each gap asks you to run.
