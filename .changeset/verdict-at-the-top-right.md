*Approve* and *Reject* move to the top right of a render, and *Approve* is the one
filled control on the page. A render whose image the run did not keep offers
neither, and says why. The build overview opens on the same count as the header,
so a changed render no component is named for reads as a render to review. Each
fact on the page is said once, cards with nothing to say are not drawn, and what
nobody measured gets one line under *Not measured*.
