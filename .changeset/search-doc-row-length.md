`ask search` weighs a word a doc holds at most two thirds as much as the same word in a name, however short the doc. A row's weight is multiplied by how much of the question it holds, each word by its rarity, so a long doc holding most of the question sorts among the first rows, above short names holding two of its words. A name with more than one row — published by more than one package, or published and also exported — is weighed through one of them, by the words its name and that row's doc hold and by that doc's length, and shown with that row: the row whose doc adds the most different words of the question to those its name holds. Of two rows that add the same set of words, the shorter doc wins, and when neither is shorter a published row; of two that add as many words but not the same ones, a published row, whatever their lengths. A name no doc adds a word to is weighed by the doc of the row it is shown with. Asked with `from` or `to`, a name is counted only through a row the section shows there: a published row a file of the area imports, or an exported row of a file in the area.
