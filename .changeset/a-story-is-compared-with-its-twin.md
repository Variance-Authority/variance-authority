`variance story --name <case> --compare twin` sets a case's readings against its twin's: the case in the same file one step toward the base of a declared `names.axes` axis, as `covering` names it. The sides are named for what each case said, such as `discount=on` against `discount=off`, and the twin is read from the record the stories were written beside. When the case has no twin, or the twin no story, its newest reading is drawn alone with the reason. `@variance-authority/sense/story` exports `recordOfStories`.
