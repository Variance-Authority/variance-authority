A record keeps a case's newest reading as what it read otherwise than the record's case index holds the case, as it already keeps each earlier reading against the newest, rather than as every region it crossed. A test file that did not finish, laid over the case's earlier crossings, now costs the regions it did not cross instead of its whole reading. A reading the index holds as it ran still costs its count alone, and one whose difference would cost more than its regions keeps its regions. Combining shards lays a newest reading again over the combined index where that index holds the case's regions otherwise than the shard's did, so it reads back intact. Two regions a case crossed under the same name, path and lines, such as a callback's entry and its body, now both read back from an earlier reading.

Upgrade every checkout that reads a record together with the CI that writes it: 0.32.0 reads a newest reading kept as a difference as the regions the index holds.
