`variance ask search` answers in fewer lines. When the repository answers, the installed names and the packages that describe your words each use one line with up to five names; a larger `--limit` shows more names while keeping those lines compact, and the line saying no installed name matched is left out; a suite whose recorded cases were not read is reported on one line, and only when the repository answered with no row; the near-match and loose headings are one short line; and an answer holding a row of this repository ends with `Next:` and the question for its first declaration, or for its first recorded test case when no declaration answered, spelled as you ask: a command in the shell, a tool and its input over MCP. Each list shows at most 20 published rows and 10 of every other kind unless `--limit` says more.
