# Journal 0032 — The rhythm between different things

**Date:** 2026-08-26

The first Underwriter reading followed the page hierarchy but stopped wherever
automatic findings stopped. That was still the wrong boundary. Five consecutive
transitions between six major content regions measured 0–0.01px, while the final
transition into activity measured 38.38px. Those regions shared a composition
owner but not a semantic shape, so repetition inference never compared their
spacing. The report remained locally correct and the investigation missed the
page-level rhythm.

The demand list was not the answer. Its 31 article boundaries were consistently
11.18–11.19px with boundary strength 0.524, distinct from the common 2px and 4px
internal spacing. Two spacing findings deeper in the report belonged to the
activity table's touching rows. Reading those findings as the page's spacing
problem would have repeated the same ownership mistake in the opposite direction.

An explicit spacing reading now accepts consecutive immediate children at one
owner. It retains the full sequence of gaps, boundary strengths and spacing
clusters, summarizes their ranges, and paints exactly those boundaries without
another acquisition. It neither requires semantic repetition nor declares a
preferred gap. The skill now asks for this reading whenever heterogeneous
siblings form one box or composition, before descending into their internal
boxes.
