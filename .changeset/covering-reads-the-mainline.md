`variance covering` reads the record `variance select` reads: in a checkout that has not recorded the suite, the mainline's at the merge base, fetched when this machine does not hold it, and the answer names that record with the same `record of "<suite>":` line, or `source` under `--format json`. When the share cannot be reached it answers from the record held here and says how far behind that is. `--since` lists what the change changed, from the merge base with `<ref>`, whatever the record holds of it: a change you committed and recorded is still your change, and `nothing has changed` means only that the merge base and the tree agree. The record's commit says how to read each file: a line stands on the line the record holds it at, a hunk from there is the change's where it meets one of the change's in the working tree, so one hunk both sides wrote is answered whole. A path the record's commit held no file by, added or renamed since, is listed with no row and says so, with `absentAt` under `--format json`, at one git process per batch of paths, a refusal in git's words when that commit does not read, and `--file` with the name that commit held reads what it recorded. A ref that does not read as a diff is one `undiffed` refusal at exit 2, before any record is read, including under `--execution`; such a ref used to be answered from the record's commit. A record made at a commit this clone lacks names what to fetch. A `--text` question, which an editor asks on every repaint, reads the records in the same order and does not fetch. A record fetched for another mainline is read by none of `covering`, `select`, `coverage`, `review`, `run`, `distill` and `journeys`, and laid by no `variance journeys` landing: each passes over that pointer to the next record this machine holds. `covering` and `select` ask the share first, and their `record of "<suite>":` line names the record they read, the primary checkout's included. `coverage` and `review` ask it only for the base they compare with, and name that base. What they read otherwise, and what `run`, `distill` and `journeys` read, is fetched by nothing and names no source. `variance ask` and the tools of `@variance-authority/mcp` do not judge by the selection, nor do `variance index`'s journey walk or a runner seam: they read through sense, which has no selection, so they take what was fetched, and `variance ask orient` can print a record `covering` refuses. The selection is a mainline's name, handed in through `selected` on `lastFetchedMainline`, `readableTestCoverage` and `prepareRecord`. `--format refs` says whose record it read on stderr. `@variance-authority/sense/test-selection` exports `cachedRecord`, `cachedSuite`, `changeInRecord`, `ChangeRead` and `absentText` and, from `@variance-authority/sense`, `existingAt`, and `CoveringChange` carries `absentAt`.

`covering --since` reads the change in the checkout `--root` names, not the directory it was asked from.
