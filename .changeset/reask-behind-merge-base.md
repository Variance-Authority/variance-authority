A mainline record behind your merge base is asked for again ten minutes after it was fetched there, so the record the mainline publishes at that merge base reaches the checkout without `variance share`. A pointer a CI job was handed by another job is marked `handed` and stands for that job without a clock, so every job of a run still reads one record.
