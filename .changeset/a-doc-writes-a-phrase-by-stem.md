`ask search` reads a doc as writing a question of two distinct stems or more when one of its paragraphs holds them in order by stem, each as many words after the last as in the question, so `page sink` finds `eventCollectorSource`, whose doc installs "the page's sink", and a name declared without being exported is read by the same rule. A function word such as `of`, or a word under three letters, holds its place, so "a page and its sink" no longer writes `page sink`, either as a match or in how near the described names sort. `phraseOfNative` answers the phrase, and `nearOfNative` counts a doc's pairs by the same rule. When nothing matches, the answer says no name or doc writes the question, no longer "as typed".
