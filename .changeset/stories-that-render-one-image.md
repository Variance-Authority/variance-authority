The docket names stories of one component that render the same image

Two stories whose candidates are byte for byte one image show nothing in a
diff: both are `new` or both `unchanged`, and both are green. Such a story is
an arm whose flag reaches no pixel, or one state under two names. A build now
carries `sameImage`, the groups of two or more stories of one component whose
candidates are stored under one key, read from the keys the build already holds.
The run uploads nothing new. A component is the part of the subject id before
the first `--`, and no group crosses one.

The docket counts these variations and links each group. A subject page lays its
component's stories side by side, in the order the journeys panel draws them, and
marks the ones that are one picture.
