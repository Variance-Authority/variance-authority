`variance story --name <name> --compare outcome` compares the runs the coverage record kept of a test when no story of it was recorded: the regions only the passing or only the failing runs reached, and each region charged to an earlier, later or no test line, or to a hook, on every failing run. A run whose file did not finish keeps its own reading in the record, not the union the index lays over it.
