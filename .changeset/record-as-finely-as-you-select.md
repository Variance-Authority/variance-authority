The selection skill and the docs now say which `precision` each recording `mode` can answer and what each mode adds to a run: `'loads'` answers `file-use`, `'entries'` answers `function-use`, and `'presence'` answers `region-use`. They also list `cadence`, `continuations`, `placement` and `unprobed` alongside them. Before this, the skill said recording was the same at every step.
