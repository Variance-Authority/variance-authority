`ask classify` ranks its areas, and `ask containment` finds the trees holding source, in the addon: the files `HEAD` holds are listed and kept in Rust, and only the ten areas, or the trees, cross to JavaScript. `@variance-authority/sense` exports `classifiedAreas` and `sourceTrees` in place of `trackedFiles`, which handed every tracked path to the caller. `Tree` in `@variance-authority/mcp/tools` gains `walk`, the addon-held walk a question over every file it reached is handed.
